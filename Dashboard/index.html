Car price prediction

Estimate used-car selling price from vehicle age, kilometres driven, model identity, fuel, seller, transmission, and owner category.

Inputs and limitations

Interpreting the score requires considering what the dataset does not measure.

Model inputs

Numeric columns are median-imputed; categories are mode-imputed and one-hot encoded. Exact duplicates are removed before splitting; IQR outliers are retained.

Why prediction is limited

Condition, trim, accident history, and location can strongly affect a sale but are not available here. The selected Lasso coefficients act on log selling price; numeric effects are conditional on the other inputs, and category effects are relative to one-hot encoded reference groups.

MAE and RMSE are in rupees; MSE squares the currency error and is scale-sensitive. R² compares explained variance to a mean-prediction baseline. A higher test R² among candidates does not override the training-CV selection rule.

03 / UNSUPERVISED LEARNING

Mall customer segmentation

Discover customer profiles from annual income and spending score. No target label is supplied to the clustering model.

Mall_Customers.csv

Data collection and processing

200 source customers → 200 valid clustering records.

5 SOURCE COLUMNS → 2 MODEL FEATURES

1 · Validate and select features

Convert annual income and spending score to numeric, then exclude rows missing either value. This dataset has 0 invalid feature rows or exact duplicates. Customer ID, age, and gender are not included in clustering.

2 · Scale and cluster

Standardize annual income and spending score so their units do not dominate distance. Inspect inertia for K=1–10 and fit the required K=5 K-Means model (random state 42, 10 initializations); assess separation with silhouette score.

Configuration and business use

K-Means · K=5 · random_state=42 · n_init=10. Income and spending score are standardized before clustering because their scales differ; the same two features appear in this plot. Customer ID, age, and gender are not model inputs.

The silhouette score of summarizes separation and cohesion (closer to 1 is better-separated; near 0 suggests overlap). A business could test segment-tailored offers or loyalty messaging, while treating these as descriptive groups rather than fixed customer identities.

Project conclusion

On this fixed holdout, Linear Regression outperformed Random Forest by R² and absolute error. The result supports a compact linear baseline for this dataset, not a claim that student performance is determined by the recorded factors. Missing predictor values were handled inside the training pipeline; test records were kept for final evaluation. Additional classroom and resource data, careful validation across cohorts, and uncertainty communication are appropriate next steps before operational use.
