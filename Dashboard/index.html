Features and interpretation

Scaling is fitted within each training pipeline.

What each measurement represents

Sepal length and width describe the flower’s outer leaf-like structures. Petal length and width describe its inner petals. The ID column is excluded from training because it is an identifier, not a biological measurement.

How the classifiers decide

Logistic Regression estimates class probabilities from scaled measurements and selects the most likely species. KNN assigns the majority label among the nearest training flowers; distance is measured after feature scaling.

Inputs and limitations

Interpreting the score requires considering what the dataset does not measure.

Model inputs

Numeric columns are median-imputed; categories are mode-imputed and one-hot encoded. Exact duplicate listings and IQR outliers were retained per the notebook decisions.

Why prediction is limited

Condition, trim, accident history, and location can strongly affect a sale but are not available here. Rare name-derived brands and extreme prices also challenge a simple linear relationship. Better vehicle condition/trim data, more careful category grouping, and justified nonlinear comparisons could help, but would need a new validation cycle.

MAE and RMSE are in rupees; MSE squares the currency error and is scale-sensitive. R² compares explained variance to a mean-prediction baseline.

Configuration and business use

K-Means · K=5 · random_state=42 · n_init=10. Income and spending score are standardized before clustering because their scales differ; the same two features appear in this plot. Customer ID, age, and gender are not model inputs.

The silhouette score of summarizes separation and cohesion (closer to 1 is better-separated; near 0 suggests overlap). A business could test segment-tailored offers or loyalty messaging, while treating these as descriptive groups rather than fixed customer identities.

Model and feature interpretation

Linear Regression fits additive coefficients; Random Forest averages decision trees to represent nonlinearities and interactions. Numeric values are median-imputed and standardized; categories are imputed and one-hot encoded inside training-fitted pipelines.

Hours studied, attendance, prior scores, tutoring, and learning access may carry predictive information. Feature importance describes how the fitted forest used encoded inputs; it does not prove a factor causes higher or lower scores.

Data quality and input coverage

Missing predictor values are imputed; rows without a target cannot be used for supervised training.

Project conclusion

On this fixed holdout, Linear Regression outperformed Random Forest by R² and absolute error. The result supports a compact linear baseline for this dataset, not a claim that student performance is determined by the recorded factors. Missing predictor values were handled inside the training pipeline; test records were kept for final evaluation. Additional classroom and resource data, careful validation across cohorts, and uncertainty communication are appropriate next steps before operational use.
